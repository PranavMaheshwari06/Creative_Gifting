# Getting customers (all free)

The website is the shop window. Most customers will find us through people they know, Instagram,
WhatsApp and Google Maps. Start these now. They don't need to wait for the website.

## WhatsApp Business (start first)
- Install the WhatsApp Business app on Mom's number.
- Add the business name, logo, area, hours and a short description.
- **Catalogue:** add the same products with photos and prices.
- **Greeting message:** "Namaste! Thank you for contacting Creative Gifting. Tell us the occasion and
  date and we'll share designs and prices."
- **Away message** for outside working hours. Quick replies for prices and delivery questions.

## Instagram
- Make a business account using the same name and logo.
- Bio: what we make, the occasions (Wedding, Shagun, Festive, Baby), our city, and "WhatsApp to order".
  Put the website link there once the site is live.
- Post 9–12 of the best photos before telling people about the account.
- After that, 2–3 posts a week is enough: finished work, short videos of packing, and happy
  customers (only with their permission).

## Google Business Profile (shows us on Google Maps)
- Create it at business.google.com with our area, phone, hours and photos.
- After every order, ask happy customers for a Google review and send them the review link.

## Photo tips
Use daylight near a window, a plain background, and one product per photo. Take a close-up of the details too.
Keep the same style every time so the page and Instagram look neat.

## Rules
- Never post fake reviews, or photos of work that isn't ours.
- Ask before showing a customer's name, face or home.
- Reply to messages quickly. That matters more than anything else on this page.
