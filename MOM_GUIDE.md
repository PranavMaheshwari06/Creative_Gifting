# Updating the website from your phone

You only need the **Creative Gifting** icon on your phone's home screen. It opens the editing page.
After you tap **Save**, the website changes in about one minute.

> Nothing you do here can break the website. If something looks wrong, just message me and I can
> put it back the way it was.

## Logging in (only the first time, or if it asks again)
1. Open the icon.
2. Type your email and tap the button to get a login link.
3. Open your email and tap the link. You're in.

## Add a new product
1. Tap **Products**, then **Add**.
2. **Photo:** tap it and take a photo or choose one from your gallery.
3. **Name:** for example "Shagun Tray – Red & Gold".
4. **Price:** just the number, for example `450`. Tick "Starting from" if the price can change.
5. **Description:** one or two lines. You can use the microphone on your keyboard to speak it.
6. Make sure **Show on website** is on.
7. Tap **Save**.

## Change a price or a photo
**Products** → tap the product → change it → **Save**.

## Remove a product from the website
**Products** → tap the product → turn **Show on website** off → **Save**.
It's only hidden, not deleted. Turn it back on to show it again.

## Change phone number, WhatsApp, Instagram or the "About" text
**Shop details** → change it → **Save**.
Type phone numbers as 10 digits, for example `9876543210`.

## Good photos
Use daylight near a window, a plain background, and one product per photo. Hold the phone steady.
